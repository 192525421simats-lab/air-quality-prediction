AQI Reference Scale & Pollution Categories

Good (0 - 50)
Minimal impact, clean and breathable air.
Moderate (51 - 100)
Acceptable air quality; minor breathing discomfort to sensitive people.
Poor (101 - 200)
Breathing discomfort to most people on prolonged exposure.
Very Poor (201 - 300)
Respiratory illness on prolonged exposure.
Severe (301+)
Affects healthy people and severely impacts those with existing conditions.
