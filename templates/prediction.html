📊 AQI Standard Classification Scale (CPCB / EPA Standards)

🌱 Good (0 - 50)
Minimal impact. Air quality is satisfactory and poses negligible or no risk.
🌿 Satisfactory (51 - 100)
Minor breathing discomfort to sensitive individuals. Generally safe for outdoors.
🌤️ Moderate (101 - 200)
Discomfort to people with asthma and heart ailments, children, and elderly.
🌫️ Poor (201 - 300)
Breathing discomfort on prolonged exposure. Limit prolonged outdoor exertion.
⚠️ Very Poor (301 - 400)
Respiratory illness on prolonged exposure. Sensitive groups should stay indoors.
🚨 Severe (401 - 500+)
Emergency condition. May cause respiratory issues even in healthy people.
